STEAL AN EGG MATH QUEST

Sneak into the lairs of mythical beasts and steal their eggs. Every guardian asks a math question first. Answer right and the egg is yours. Answer wrong and SMACK! you're sent flying back to base.

CHOOSE YOUR MATH LEVEL

How to play: Move with WASD, turn the camera with ←→ or by dragging, Space to jump, scroll to zoom (on touch: thumbstick on the left, drag to look, JUMP button on the right). Walk up to an egg and press E (or tap it). Bring stolen eggs back to your base to hatch creatures that earn cash. Stand on the treadmill to build speed and unlock deeper zones. Rarer eggs mean harder questions. Every 5 minutes night falls: eggs restock, base eggs hatch faster, and a rare ✨ Divine egg may appear until sunrise. The lair egg at the back of each zone is the rarest. Squeeze through rubble gaps the monster can't fit through and hit the green boost pads to escape. The more a creature earns, the bigger it grows.
